REV D2 — J1, J2, J3 ASSEMBLY ORIENTATION (RETAINED FROM D1)

Part: XINYA / Ningbo Xinlaiya XY350V-3.5-4P, JLC/LCSC C557656.
Install all three terminal blocks on the top side.
WIRE ENTRY OPENINGS FACE THE LOWER BOARD EDGE, labelled inputs 1 through 12.
SCREW HEADS FACE UP, accessible from above the PCB.
Do not mount with wire openings facing the components in the board interior.

Board coordinates: X increases right, Y increases down.
Board outline: X=0..86 mm, Y=0..62 mm.
Terminal body centres: J1=(32,52), J2=(52,52), J3=(72,52) mm.
Wire entry direction: +Y, toward the Y=62 mm edge.
Body edge on entry side: Y=55.45 mm. Body height: 8.6 mm above PCB.
Native KiCad CPL rotation: 180 degrees for each terminal.
JLC library zero angles may differ; the required physical facing direction
above takes precedence over a numeric rotation or a mismounted 3D model.

Input labels stay in ascending order left-to-right when viewed from above:
J1: 1,2,3,4; J2: 5,6,7,8; J3: 9,10,11,12.
Library contact numbers within each block are 4,3,2,1 left-to-right.
The square contact-1 pads are J1=(37.25,52), J2=(57.25,52), J3=(77.25,52).
Those pads carry P4, P8, P12 respectively.
The manufacturer drawing does not number contacts; contact 1 is our CAD datum.
