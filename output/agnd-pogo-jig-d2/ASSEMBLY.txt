REV D2 AGND POGO CALIBRATION JIG — V2 DROP-IN FRAME

PRINT FILES
D2_AGND_jig_drop_in.3mf     : frame, arm and optional fit coupon, arranged flat.
01_base.stl                : print one, 100 x 84 x 27 mm.
02_pogo_arm.stl            : print one, 18 x 27 x 10 mm.
03_pin_fit_coupon.stl      : optional; 2.0 / 2.1 / 2.2 / 2.3 / 2.4 mm bores.

All dimensions are millimetres. Print at 100% scale with the supplied flat faces
on the bed. The complete 3MF layout occupies 125 x 85 mm. The 3MF contains mesh
geometry and layout; choose your own printer and filament profile in the slicer.

Starting print settings: PETG, 0.20 mm layers, four walls, five top/bottom layers,
40% infill. PLA is also usable for a room-temperature prototype. The parts are
designed to print without supports; the small screw bores and nut pockets use
short bridges. Remove any loose strands from bores and nut pockets after printing.
Print the coupon first to check the barrel fit. The arm has a nominal 2.20 mm bore
for the 2.00 mm barrel, closed gently by the split clamp. Clean the bore until the
barrel can slide freely before tightening. Do not force the spring mechanism.

HARDWARE (plain hex nuts; locking nuts do not fit these pockets)
No screws, nuts or locating pegs are used to mount the PCB. It rests in the frame.
2 x M3 x 16 mm machine screws and 2 x M3 nuts — arm to rear pedestal.
1 x M3 x 12 mm machine screw and 1 x M3 nut — pogo split clamp.
1 x supplied pogo pin: 2.0 mm barrel, 1.5 mm contact tip,
    33.0 mm extended total length, 25.7 mm fully compressed total length.
1 x insulated wire from the stationary pogo tail to calibration supply negative.

Nut pocket dimensions, for checking your hardware:
M3 arm mounting: 5.80 mm wide x 2.60 mm high, entered from the rear of pedestal.
M3 clamp: 5.80 mm across flats x 2.70 mm thick, entered from the arm's right side.
The screw lengths above assume no washers. Tighten the plastic fixture by hand.

ASSEMBLY
1. Switch off the bench supply and disconnect USB before installing the PCB.
   Input 1's completed calibration has been saved in device memory.
2. With the pogo arm removed, lower the PCB into the frame. The green input
   terminals face the REV D2 engraving on the front; the tall pedestal sits
   behind the PCB's upper-right corner. The board rests on a 2 mm wide edge
   ledge. The small additional support lies directly beneath the AGND pad.
   The pocket is 86.4 x 62.4 mm: 0.2 mm clearance on EACH side of the board.
   Four 4 mm diameter circular corner reliefs clear the PCB's sharp corners.
   A 0.6 mm x 45 degree chamfer guides the PCB into the opening. Do not scale
   the model in the slicer to change the clearance; that also shifts the pin.
3. Insert two M3 nuts into the pedestal's rear slots. Place the arm on its two
   circular locating bosses, AGND lettering upward. Install two M3 x 16 screws.
4. Insert the clamp's M3 nut through the right side slot. Start the M3 x 12 clamp
   screw from the arm's front face, leaving the clamp loose.
5. Feed the pogo pin downward through the arm, contact tip first. Its tip must
   land on TP19 / AGND: the uppermost pad in the group at the right edge, below
   and left of the PCB's upper-right mounting hole. Check the contact visually.
6. Let the tip just touch the pad with the spring extended. Mark this position,
   then slide the stationary barrel down about 2.0 mm and gently tighten the
   split clamp around the barrel. Check that the moving plunger remains free.
   At this setting, pin total length is about 31.0 mm, and its rear end is about
   6.6 mm above the arm's top face. Never set the pin at its fully compressed stop.
   If solder or a wire changes the visible rear-end length, use the 2 mm movement
   from first contact rather than the exposed-tail dimension.
7. Connect the stationary tail to the calibration supply NEGATIVE. Secure the
   wire to the pedestal so pulling the wire does not change pin insertion depth.
   The positive supply lead moves between numbered inputs during calibration.
   Keep this AGND connection separate from USB_GND.

AFTER CALIBRATION
Remove the pogo arm / AGND connection before normal floating any-pair testing.
The board can remain seated in the frame. The two arm screws allow arm removal
without changing the clamp's insertion-depth setting. Lift the PCB out with the
arm removed. This is a gravity-seated frame, with no snap clips or lid.

DESIGN BASIS / FIT STATUS
Board: actual Rev D2 KiCad source, 86 x 62 x 1.6 mm.
PCB mounting holes are left unused.
Pocket: 86.4 x 62.4 mm below the entry chamfer, giving 0.2 mm per-side clearance.
Corner reliefs: 4 mm diameter, centred at the four pocket corners, through the base.
Entry chamfer: 0.6 mm at 45 degrees, including around the circular corner reliefs.
Edge ledge: 2 mm inside the nominal board outline, at z=9 mm.
Frame lip: z=11.4 mm; the 1.6 mm PCB's top sits at z=10.6 mm.
AGND / TP19: (77,10) mm, 1.5 mm diameter pad.
Coordinates above are measured from the PCB's top-left corner, x right, y down.
AGND is 6 mm left and 7 mm below the upper-right mounting-hole centre.
PCB underside sits 9 mm above the jig bottom; base plate thickness is 4 mm.
The arm bottom is 25 mm above the jig bottom. Pin insertion depth is adjustable.

The source CAD and exported meshes passed solid/manifold/closed-mesh checks.
The two printed parts and the seated PCB envelope have zero nominal interference.
Wall, ledge, corner relief and chamfer sections are checked in the CAD generator.
The pogo bore is centred on the AGND coordinate. The preview uses the actual D2 board model;
fasteners, wire and the pin's shoulder/tail proportions are simplified.
This is a first-print design. Physical fit, your printer's clearances, and pogo
spring force have not been tested. Start with the fit coupon and check the contact
and clamp freedom before applying power.
