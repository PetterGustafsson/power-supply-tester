ENCLOSURE PLANNING — REV D2 ASSEMBLY

These renders combine the actual Rev D2 KiCad board geometry with the official
Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.28 STEP model. The non-touch display module contains
the ESP32; a separate ESP32 board is not shown or required by this architecture.
The screen content is the latest connector UI artwork from the other project
chat: output/silk-colors-clean-preview.png, copied unchanged into
sources/display-connector-ui.png and packed into the new Blender files.
It uses violet, amber and coral voltage groups, molded pin numerals and cyan G,
with no black plates behind the voltage labels. The 5/12/24 V values are simulated
artwork, not live readings or validation of the Rev D2 measurement range.

FILES
board-and-display-usb-rear-connector-ui.png / .blend: open assembly with a proposed five-wire harness.
lid-mounted-layout-usb-rear-connector-ui.png / .blend: display above the PCB in a proposed lid position.
The display module is rotated 180 degrees so USB points toward the rear, away
from the screw terminals. The illustrative screen remains upright; firmware
display rotation will need to match this mounting orientation. Firmware was not
modified for these assembly views. Earlier files preserve the previous views.

DIMENSIONS
Main PCB: 86 x 62 x 1.6 mm.
PCB mounting holes: 2.7 mm diameter, at (3,3), (83,3), (3,59), (83,59) mm
relative to the board's top-left corner in the PCB editor.
Display PCB: 36.5 mm wide x 39.5 mm overall, including the USB tab.
Visible LCD active area: 32.4 mm diameter. This is not a finished cutout dimension.
Official model overall thickness including rear connectors: approximately 7.9 mm.
Starting external enclosure envelope: 96 x 76 x 42 mm, provisional.

MECHANICAL STATUS
PCB components and display body are source CAD. Wires, mating-plug envelopes,
USB plug, standoffs and case outline are illustrative planning geometry.
There is no finished enclosure, mounting system, strain relief or cutout design.
The enclosure envelope has not been fit-checked against purchased plugs or parts.
Allow access to terminal wire entries and the screws from above, and leave room
for wire bends behind the display and for its USB-C plug. The pictured straight
USB plug and cable are generic; their dimensions vary between cables.
The display has no screw mounting holes; use a bezel/cradle and rear retainer
that support the module without loading the glass. Provide access to its buttons.
The display-side header pitch is 1.27 mm. The tester-side J4 pitch is 2.5 mm;
the five-wire link is a custom harness, not a matching connector at both ends.

WIRE FUNCTIONS — VERIFY PIN ORIENTATION BEFORE ASSEMBLING A CABLE
J4 pin 1: USB5V -> display VSYS (red in the concept).
J4 pin 2: USB_GND -> display GND (black).
J4 pin 3: HOST3V3 -> display 3V3 (orange).
J4 pin 4: SDA_HOST -> display GPIO6 (yellow).
J4 pin 5: SCL_HOST -> display GPIO7 (blue).
Wire colors are a proposed convention. The render is not a cable fabrication drawing.
